Every power plant that reported to the U.S. Energy Information Administration in the last ten years — including the ones that have since shut down. Pick a state, then a plant, to see what it generated every month, what it burned, and an estimate of the CO₂ that came with it.

Start by choosing a state

The second menu then lists every plant in that state that reported to EIA from 2016 onward, with its main energy source in brackets. Plants that have stopped reporting are listed separately, so retired coal and nuclear units are still here with their full history.

  • Monthly net generation by energy source, for up to ten years plus this year to date
  • Capacity factor, heat rate and rank among the state's plants
  • Estimated CO₂ every month, and how carbon-intensive each megawatt-hour was
  • Every generator at the plant: technology, size, online date and planned retirement
