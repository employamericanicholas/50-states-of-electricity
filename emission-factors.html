Every CO₂ figure in the dashboard is an estimate built from the bottom up: for each plant and each fuel it burned, the fuel consumed in million Btu multiplied by the factor below. These are EIA's published factors, applied at EIA's finest fuel granularity — bituminous, subbituminous and lignite coal each carry their own value rather than a coal average.
